Theralert is the web app I'm actively developing. It acts as a notification
service and calendar that lets nursing homes and assisted-living facilities
alert family members to the activities of their patients.

- Built the backend in **Go** for a fast, lightweight notification service.
- **Containerized with Docker** and self-hosted on a home lab so facilities can
  register and start using it directly.
