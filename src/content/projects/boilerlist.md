BoilerList is a student marketplace built on the **MERN stack** (MongoDB,
Express, React, Node).

- Created a secure, centralized platform for Purdue students to buy and sell
  their items.
- Focused on a clean, user-friendly experience for listing and discovering
  goods on campus.
